Hello, world! We just kicked off our Scrum Team for the dlp (DLP/Digital Library Program) effort, starting development on our first two products, Curate and “Disco” (pending its final name). Part of our startup work also involves getting our Scrum ducks in a row, and we’ve been scaffolding our internal processes and understanding of critical work entities into some very interesting outputs: glossary/definitions, entity hierarchies, process maps, tool recommendations, and templates.
